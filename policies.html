Privacy Policy

Cifelli's Pizza (700 Chews Landing Rd, Lindenwold, NJ 08021) runs this website and its online ordering system. Here's what we collect, why, and where it's stored.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, phone number, and — for delivery — your address. If you check out with a card, that's handled by whoever's ringing you up at pickup or delivery; we don't collect or store card numbers through this website (see "How you pay," below).
  • If you create an optional account: the email and password you choose, used only to show you your own order history and saved preferences when you sign back in. Ordering has never required an account — a phone number is all that's needed either way.
  • Automatically: basic, anonymous traffic counts via Cloudflare Web Analytics (page views, roughly how people found the site) — no cookies, no cross-site tracking, and it can't identify you personally. Your browser's own local storage is used to remember your cart between visits and whether you've seen the homepage intro before; this stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Where it's stored

Order and account data lives in a Supabase-hosted database (Supabase is a database/backend provider — think of it as the filing cabinet, not a separate company we hand your information to for their own use). Access is restricted by row-level security: only shop staff can see order details, and a signed-in customer can only ever see their own past orders, never anyone else's.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information to anyone.
  • We don't share it with third parties except the services that make the site work at all (Supabase for the database, Cloudflare for basic traffic stats, and the free map/routing services noted under "Delivery & Location Data" below) — none of whom use it for their own marketing.

Questions, corrections, or removal requests

This is a small, single-location shop — there's no automated "download my data" or "delete my account" button yet. If you'd like a copy of what we have on file, want something corrected, or want your account and order history removed, call or stop by and we'll take care of it directly.

Ordering, Cancellation & Refunds

How you pay

Every order here — online, by phone, or walking in — is paid in person, cash or card, at pickup or when your delivery arrives. Placing an online order does not charge anything; it's a ticket that goes straight to the kitchen, the same as if you'd called it in.

Changing or cancelling an order

Once an order is placed, the fastest way to change or cancel it is to call the shop directly at (856) 435-8799 — there's no self-service cancel button on the ordering page, since by the time an order shows up in the kitchen, someone's often already started on it. The sooner you call, the more likely it hasn't been started yet.

Refunds

Since nothing is charged when you place an online order, there's rarely anything to "refund" in the credit-card sense — if something's wrong with your order (wrong item, mistake on our end, it never showed up), tell us at pickup/delivery or call, and we'll make it right on the spot: remake it, adjust what you owe, or come to a fair resolution over the phone. We don't have a formal written refund policy beyond that, because for a pay-in-person shop it's mostly a conversation, not a form.

Text Message Consent

The order form has an optional checkbox to be texted when your order's ready. Checking it means you're opting in to receive that one kind of message — an order-status text — nothing else; we don't send marketing texts. Standard message and data rates from your own carrier may apply. You're never required to opt in to place an order.

(Technical note, for anyone curious: this opt-in is recorded today, but the text-sending itself isn't switched on yet — see this project's own build notes if you're the site owner reading this before that's finished. Nothing changes about what opting in means to you once it is.)

Delivery & Location Data

Your delivery address

When you enter a delivery address and tap "Check Address," it's sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim (to find the location) and OSRM (to estimate driving distance/time) — both free, public mapping services, used only to show you an estimated distance and give our driver a sense of the route. Your address is also stored with your order so kitchen and delivery staff know where it's going.

Driver location

While a driver has your delivery out, the app tracks that driver's live location so staff can see deliveries in progress on an internal map. This is scoped tightly: a driver's phone only reports location while they actually have an active delivery — never continuously, never when they're off shift or between deliveries — and that location is visible only to shop staff, never to customers, and is deleted once the delivery is complete. If you're a driver reading this: this is the same thing described in the app itself, not a separate hidden system.
